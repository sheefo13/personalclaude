How to Run an Arab Graduation Game Night

Already have the game? Here's how to make the party memorable:

  1. Clear a table after the food. The mansaf and kibbeh keep people around — use that momentum.
  2. Mix generations intentionally. Put teta on a team with the youngest cousin. The chaos is the point.
  3. Set the stakes. Loser does the dishes. Winner picks the next gathering location. Whatever gets people competitive.
  4. Record it. Arab family game nights belong on TikTok. The arguing alone is content.
  5. Have the Meme Edition ready as round two. Once the trivia gets heated, switch to captions and watch things escalate.
