# UGC Influencer Outreach — The Arab Card Game

## Strategy Notes
- Micro-influencers (5k–100k followers) respond better to DMs than email
- Don't mention follower counts, metrics, or deliverables in first contact
- Lead with something specific about their content — not a generic pitch
- Offer is simple: free game, no obligation, share if you love it
- Follow up once after 5 days if no response. Then move on.

---

## Master DM Script (Instagram / TikTok)

> Hey [Name]! Love your content — [one specific genuine line about something they posted].
>
> I run The Arab Card Game — it's a card game made for Arab families and the diaspora. Trivia edition tests if you're "Really Arab Bro," meme edition has everyone calling out their cousin.
>
> Would love to send you both editions, no strings attached. If you play it with family or friends and love it, amazing. If not, no worries at all — keep the games either way.
>
> Let me know if you want me to send over details!

**Personalization notes:**
- Replace the bracketed line with something specific to their content
- Don't say "collab," "partnership," or "promotion" in first message
- Keep it under 5 sentences

## Tracking Status Key
- **Ready** — have email, draft created in Gmail
- **Research** — need to find email or DM handle
- **Contacted** — message sent, waiting for response
- **Following Up** — sent follow-up
- **Interested** — responded positively
- **Shipped** — games sent
- **Posted** — they shared content
- **Pass** — not interested or no response after follow-up
