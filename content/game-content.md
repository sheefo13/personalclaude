## Brand Voice
- Funny first. Always.
- The arguments are the feature, not a warning
- Arab diversity is the punchline — 22 countries, zero consensus
- Bridges generations: immigrants vs. born-here, parents vs. kids, teta vs. everyone
- The meme edition hits too close to home — that recognition IS the humor
- Uses Arabic words naturally: 3zooma, habibi, inshallah, yalla, wallah, shibshib, zaghrouta
- Tagline: "Are you Really Arab Bro?"
- Never talks down to the audience — speaks as one Arab to another
